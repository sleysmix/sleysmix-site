# About Sleysmix

When Kingsley Okoro moved to Houston, he was working two jobs and still living paycheck to paycheck. In his words, it felt like being "in quicksand sinking slowly." A neighbor suggested he try bartending and got him hired at a Houston sports bar franchise. That became job number three, and it raised a bigger question: why not do this for himself?

That question became Sleysmix. Kingsley is TABC certified and built Sleysmix as a mobile bar service for corporate events, nonprofits, weddings and private celebrations across Greater Houston, from Pearland to The Woodlands. As the events added up, Kingsley realized Sleysmix was about more than bartending. He loves bringing people together and making a difference for the people at the event, not just the event itself. Every event starts with a conversation, so the drink menu fits your guests and your occasion.
